Privacy Policy

Compety

Last updated: 28 August 2026

Ver en español

Compety is a sports competition app for groups of friends. It reads physical activity data from your iPhone, calculates a score, and shares that score with members of the leagues you join. This policy explains what data is handled, where it is handled, and what leaves your phone.

The short version. Your health data is read and processed on your own iPhone. Only your score travels to the server, never your heart rate, your sleep or your workouts.

1. Data controller

The controller is Manuel Parraga, an individual based in Spain, trading as Pizco Deploy.

  • Contact: pizcodeploy@gmail.com

2. Health data: what is read and where it stays

With your explicit authorisation, Compety reads the following from the Health app on your iPhone (HealthKit):

DataPurposeLeaves the phone
Workouts: type, duration and timeKnow what activity you didNo
Heart rate during those workoutsCalculate the effort of the sessionNo
Active energy, distance and stepsSession contextNo
Sleep and daily metrics (resting heart rate, oxygen saturation, respiratory rate, VO₂ max, heart rate variability)Show you your own trendNo
Your scoreLeague standingsYes

Score calculation happens entirely on your device. Raw health data is not transmitted to any server, is not stored outside your iPhone, and is not copied to iCloud.

Compety does not write any data to the Health app.

3. Where the data comes from

Compety does not connect to your tracker or watch. It only reads what other apps have saved into the Health app, whether that is the iPhone itself, an Apple Watch, or apps such as Google Health, WHOOP, Garmin Connect, Oura, Strava or Nike Run Club.

You enable that sync inside each of those apps, and it is governed by their own privacy policies, which are independent from this one.

4. Account data

Competing with other people requires an account. The following data is processed:

  • Sign-in identifier: your email address, or the identifier provided by Sign in with Apple. If you use Sign in with Apple you can choose to hide your real address, in which case we only receive an anonymous relay address from Apple.
  • Display name: whatever you choose to appear as in standings. It can be a nickname and you can change it any time.
  • Leagues you belong to and the scores you have earned.

Date of birth, gender, phone number, postal address and identity documents are never requested.

5. What other people can see

Members of a league you have joined can see your display name, your score and your position in that league.

They cannot see your heart rate, your sleep, your health metrics or the detail of your sessions. That data never leaves your phone, so it is not even available to share.

Leagues are only accessible with an invitation code. There is no public user directory and no people search.

6. Contacts, location and advertising

Compety does not access your contacts, your location, your photos, your microphone or your camera.

Compety shows no advertising and does not use health data for advertising, marketing or commercial profiling. It is not sold or shared with third parties for those purposes.

8. Legal basis and retention

Processing of health data relies on your explicit consent (GDPR article 9.2.a), granted in the system permission sheet and withdrawable at any time from your iPhone Settings.

Processing of account data relies on performance of the service you request (article 6.1.b).

Account data and scores are kept while your account remains open. Deleting the account deletes them.

9. Your rights

You can exercise access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection rights by writing to pizcodeploy@gmail.com.

Delete your account: you can do it directly in the app, under Settings, without contacting anyone. Your account, display name, scores and league memberships are removed.

Withdraw health data access: iPhone Settings, Privacy and Security, Health, Compety. The app keeps working, though without data it cannot calculate your score.

If you believe the processing does not comply with the applicable rules, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency.

10. Children

Compety is not directed at children under 16 and does not knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, notice will be given inside the app before the change takes effect. The last update date appears at the top.
