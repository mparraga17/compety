2. What the app does

Compety reads physical activity data from the Health app on your iPhone, calculates an effort score per session, and compares it with other people inside private leagues you join with an invitation code.

The purpose is to encourage physical activity through friendly competition.

4. The numbers you see are approximations

Scores are calculated from data generated by third-party trackers, watches and apps, and those devices have known margins of error. Optical heart rate sensors lose accuracy as exercise intensity rises, and each brand measures differently.

For that reason:

  • Scores are normalised against your own historical baseline, not against other people's absolute values.
  • When a session has no heart rate, the score is estimated from the activity type and its duration, and the app says so visibly.
  • A missing value is not a zero. It may mean you were not wearing the device, or that a permission is missing.

The score is an indicative measure of effort, not a clinical measurement.
